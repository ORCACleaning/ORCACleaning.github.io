PERTH BY THE NUMBERS

What 5,438 recently reviewed Perth listings reveal

Last reviewed: 18 November 2026

A Western Host analysis of public Airbnb listing data for the 30 Perth metropolitan councils: how many listings were recently reviewed, how many are whole homes, what they ask per night, and where they are.

5,438Airbnb listings in the Perth metropolitan region with at least one review in the 12 months before the data was collected on 22–23 September 2026 (out of 6,268 listed).
76%share of those recently reviewed listings that are entire homes or apartments.
A$296median advertised nightly price, from 5,071 recently reviewed listings with a usable price (A$350 for entire homes, from 3,845 listings).

Top five councils by recently reviewed listings

Stirling 762 · Fremantle 544 · Perth 525 · Joondalup 333 · Canning 271

Within the recently reviewed Airbnb listings analysed, the largest counts were recorded in Stirling, Fremantle and the City of Perth. Of the 5,438 Airbnb listings in the Perth metropolitan region that received at least one review in the year before the data was collected in late September 2026, the City of Stirling had 762, followed by Fremantle (544), the City of Perth (525), Joondalup (333) and Canning (271).

Three in four of these listings were entire homes or apartments. Under WA planning rules, unhosted short-stay accommodation in metropolitan Perth generally requires development approval when operated beyond the 90-night exemption (see page 6). An Airbnb listing classified as an entire home is not necessarily unhosted under WA law; its actual circumstances determine the applicable requirements.

The median advertised price was A$296 a night, calculated from 5,071 listings with usable prices, and A$350 for entire homes (3,845 listings). These are the nightly prices Airbnb quoted for near-term stays, not what guests actually paid, and the data does not say whether they include GST. One quoted price above A$10,000 a night was excluded as an extreme outlier.

The figures cover Airbnb listings only, not other platforms, and they are not a count of registered properties. A current official WA register total was not available to us.

Key point: Three in four recently reviewed Airbnb listings were entire homes or apartments. That does not establish how many are legally classified as unhosted accommodation.

Source and method

Western Host analysis of Inside Airbnb's Western Australia listing data, dated 21 September 2026, listings collected 22 to 23 September 2026 (insideairbnb.com). Licensed under CC BY 4.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0); this is a modified analysis: we filtered and analysed the original data. Perth metropolitan region: the 30 local governments of the Metropolitan Region Scheme, as named in the WA Planning Commission's Perth and Peel @3.5million documents (Mandurah, Murray and Waroona excluded). Recently reviewed: at least one review in the 12 months to the collection date. Prices: Airbnb's quoted nightly price for a near-term stay; blanks removed and one extreme outlier above A$10,000 a night excluded. GST status not stated. Airbnb listings only; not the whole short-stay market.
