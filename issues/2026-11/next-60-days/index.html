NEXT 60 DAYS · 1 DECEMBER 2026 – 31 JANUARY 2027, PLUS LOOK-AHEAD

Perth's summer dates worth planning around

Last reviewed: 17 November 2026

From Boxing Day cricket to school holidays and the opening of Fringe World, Perth's summer calendar brings dates worth watching. Here is what to check when planning rates, staffing and guest arrivals.

Events that move demand

  • Now on: Lotterywest Films season at Somerville Auditorium, UWA, running to 21 March 2027.
  • 20 December 2026: Perth Scorchers v Hobart Hurricanes, Optus Stadium.
  • 26 December 2026: Perth Scorchers v Melbourne Stars, Optus Stadium.
  • 30 December 2026: Perth Scorchers v Sydney Thunder, Optus Stadium.
  • 31 December 2026: New Year's Eve, peak night across Perth.
  • 4 January 2027: Perth Scorchers v Brisbane Heat, Optus Stadium.
  • 13 January 2027: Perth Scorchers v Melbourne Renegades, Optus Stadium.
  • 20 January 2027: Fringe World opens, Perth (runs to 14 February 2027).
  • 26 January 2027: Australia Day, public holiday.

Look-ahead: just beyond the 60 days

  • 1 February 2027: school Term 1 begins; family demand eases.

Deadlines and dates

  • 17 December 2026: final day of Term 4 for WA public schools; summer school holidays follow.
  • 25 December 2026: Christmas Day, public holiday.
  • 26 and 28 December 2026: Boxing Day (Saturday) and its substitute public holiday (Monday).
  • 1 January 2027: New Year's Day, public holiday.
  • Any time: renew your STRA registration before its 12-month expiry (A$100).

Seasonal checks

  • Bushfire preparedness: check Emergency WA for current warnings and total fire bans, and your local government's published burning restrictions. Requirements vary by location.

Host's planning notes

Check minimum stays around peak dates, confirm cleaner availability for public holidays, review guest arrival arrangements, and compare event dates with your own booking history before changing rates.

  • Through the peak: watch your let-nights against the 90-night exemption as summer fills.
Key point: five Scorchers home matches fall between 20 December and 13 January. Check how these dates affect bookings in your area before changing rates or staffing.
Sources: perthfestival.com.au; fringeworld.com.au; education.wa.edu.au; wa.gov.au; planning.wa.gov.au
