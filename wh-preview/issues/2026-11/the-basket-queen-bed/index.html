THE BASKET · BEDROOM SET-UP

What it costs to dress one queen bed

Last reviewed: 17 November 2026

For Perth and WA hosts: we priced one queen bedroom's sheets, quilt, pillows, protector, towels and amenities from supplier product pages on 17 November 2026. Here is what each set-up costs, how GST changes the sums, and who delivers.

10 min read

We priced the essential linen and bedding for one queen bedroom using Australian retail and hospitality-supplier listings on 17 November 2026: sheets, a queen quilt and cover, two pillows, a mattress protector and towels. The retail selection came to A$347.04 and the hospitality-supplier selection to A$354.87, before GST is put on one consistent basis. Amenities, spare sets and delivery are extra. Repeat it for every bed in the property, then add spare sets of the washable items, and the opening bill grows quickly.

Every price below was read from the supplier's own page that day; one, flagged in the table, comes from a listing page because the product page showed no price. Where a page would not load, we left the item out rather than estimate it.

How to compare the totals. The retail selection totals A$347.04 at the displayed marketplace prices, where GST is not stated. The hospitality selection totals A$354.87 using Reward Hospitality's ex-GST prices. Using Reward's displayed inc-GST prices instead, it comes to an estimated A$382 to A$390, depending on whether the A$80.00 BNB Supplies quilt-cover price includes GST. Because GST treatment, towel quantities (the retail hand towels come as a four-pack) and product specifications differ, these figures show two purchasing options, not a strict like-for-like comparison. Delivery is excluded.

Hospitality suppliers were cheaper on the selected sheet and towel lines (A$61.96 ex GST against A$106.20 for sheets), although the towel quantities differed. The selected commercial quilt, cover, pillows and mattress protector were more expensive. National Hotel Supplies listed a 160 gsm polycotton queen set at A$131.95, tax included, so hospitality prices vary between suppliers as much as they differ from retail.

How to choose

Price is the smaller variable. Durability is the larger one, and no price table shows it. Hospitality sheets are commonly polycotton, a cotton and polyester blend built for frequent hot washing: the Reward queen sheets we priced are 135 gsm and 50/50 polycotton. Retail sets often lead with a high thread count, which says little about how the fabric survives a commercial wash. Read the care label for the highest wash temperature and whether tumble drying is allowed, then wash one set a few times before ordering more.

White sheets and towels make matching, replacement and spotting marks simpler, but colour alone does not decide how well linen survives washing. Check the manufacturer's care instructions for allowed temperatures, bleaching and tumble drying before choosing linen for repeated commercial laundering.

Budget for three sets of linen per bed: one on the bed, one in the wash and one on the shelf. That is a common industry rule of thumb, not an official standard, and a property with same-day changeovers may need more.

What a starter set costs (17 November 2026)

One queen bedroom, linen and towels only:

  • Retail (Bunnings Marketplace, GST not stated): A$347.04.
  • Hospitality suppliers (Reward Hospitality ex GST, plus one BNB Supplies line with GST not stated): A$354.87; about A$382 to A$390 including GST.

Add amenities:

  • Retail route, a 5-litre shampoo refill from Reward at A$27.29 ex GST (you also need dispensers, which we did not price): A$374.33 in total.
  • Hospitality route, a carton of 300 single-use 30 ml shampoo tubes at A$128.99 ex GST, about A$0.43 a tube: A$483.86 in total.

Amenities are a separate purchasing decision. The 5-litre refill and the 300-tube carton are different dispensing formats, so compare cost per millilitre, packaging, dispenser needs and waste before choosing.

What this means for WA hosts

Compare totals on one GST basis, not single unit prices. Ask each supplier whether a quoted price includes GST, whether a trade account is needed, and what delivery to Perth costs, because several sites did not say. Buy one hospitality set, wash it a few times your usual way, and judge it before ordering for the whole property.
