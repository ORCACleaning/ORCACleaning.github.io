About

Free, monthly, and written for WA short-stay operators

Western Host is a free monthly magazine for Perth and WA short-stay hosts, owners, co-hosts and property managers. We take the rules as they stand on a stated date, check them against the official source, and explain what they mean in plain words.

From the editor

Welcome to the first issue

Western Australia's short-stay market has spent two years being rewritten. Registration became compulsory on 1 January 2025. From 1 January 2026, properties had to show they meet planning requirements to stay registered. In August, Consumer Protection announced the first infringement under the new laws, over an advertisement allegedly missing its registration number.

For anyone running a short stay, or managing them for owners, that adds up to a lot of rules and not much time. This is the first issue of Western Host, and it exists to close that gap. Each month we take the rules as they stand on a stated date, check them against the official source, and explain what they mean in plain words. We add the numbers, the costs and the dates that help you run a better business.

This issue starts where every host should: registration, the number in every listing, and the new Airbnb rule on what guests can be asked to do at checkout. Then the money: what booking platforms now report to the ATO, and what one queen bed really costs to dress. And with summer here, a 25-point check to do before the next guest arrives.

If something here is wrong, tell us and we will correct it in print. If there is a question you want answered, send it in. We read everything.

— Behzad Bagheri, Editor

How we work

  • Every rule, figure and price is checked against its source and dated.
  • Laws in force are kept separate from proposals.
  • Questions in Ask Western Host come from official FAQs until readers send their own; we never invent letters.
  • Some images in this publication are AI-generated or digitally altered illustrations. They are illustrative only and do not document actual people, properties, events or services.
