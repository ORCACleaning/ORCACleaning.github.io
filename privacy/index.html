Privacy

Privacy policy

How Western Host collects and uses your information, and the choices you control.

Who we are

Western Host is published by GG Investors Pty Ltd (ABN 57 682 794 047), Perth, Western Australia. You can contact us at westernhost@gginvestors.com.au.

What we collect

When you subscribe we collect your email address, your first name if you choose to give it, the consent choices you tick, the time you signed up, the page you signed up from, and the time you confirm your subscription.

Why we collect it

We use your email to send you what you asked for: the monthly magazine, and, only if you tick the separate box, occasional marketing about ORCA services. Your second consent is stored separately so you can withdraw it on its own without unsubscribing from the magazine.

Who we share it with

We never sell or give our subscriber list to advertisers. We use Sender (sender.net) as our email provider to store subscriptions and send the magazine. We do not add anyone to our list who did not sign up themselves.

Unsubscribe, access, correction and deletion

Every email has a working unsubscribe link, and we act on it within 5 working days. You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by emailing westernhost@gginvestors.com.au.

How to complain

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

This policy is written in plain language and describes our current practice. Current to 17 November 2026.
